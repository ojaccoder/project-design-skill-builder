# Official-site observations

Checked 2026-10-26 with a real Chrome browser at 1440×1000. Pages change; treat these as dated observations. The reusable methods in platform-design-patterns.md are our synthesis, not measured conversion or performance findings. No source branding, media or copy is licensed for reuse by this reference.

## 千问开放平台
Source: https://open.qianwen.com/home
Observed: centered headline and prominent application CTA; a saturated blue primary access panel beside a subdued unavailable panel; a video demonstration; a pale colored capability region with unequal tile proportions. Each group communicates a different content level.
Adaptation: communicate priority and availability through contrast; move from a concise promise into actual product evidence; vary region composition while retaining brand continuity.
Do not import its claims, logos, exact text or business onboarding structure. A 390×844 inspection showed clipped first-screen content despite no document-level horizontal overflow; inspect actual rendering, not just scrollWidth, and do not reproduce that defect.

## ByteDance Seed
Source: https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/
Observed: a broad media-led model stage with adjacent content visible, model selectors below, updates, and a later product demonstration region. In a follow-up visit a product UI video played (currentTime advanced); a model-selector click initiated horizontal stage movement. The initial visit captured an unloaded hero surface, so a single screenshot was insufficient to judge media behavior.
Adaptation: give product evidence enough space, use scene selection for related capabilities, and give media a reliable loading/static state. This does not justify autoplay or a carousel for every website.

## 火山引擎
Source: https://www.volcengine.com/
Observed: a concise centered hero and single build CTA, a visual content strip with previous/next controls, a choice between model selection and enterprise scenarios, category tabs, and a denser product catalog. The artificial-intelligence category was clicked successfully. DOM inspection found canvas/video elements; that alone does not establish performance quality.
Adaptation: keep the entry simple while exposing richer discovery below; use categories and scenarios to organize density; choose a small subset appropriate to the project instead of copying a cloud catalog or floating sales widgets.

## 字节跳动公司官网
Source: https://www.bytedance.com/zh/
Observed: blue corporate navigation; oversized curved visual fields and a compact mission block; later culture content and a timeline. Visual scale changes between sections.
Adaptation: carry a recognizable brand shape across section boundaries and change content rhythm for brand storytelling. Its corporate mission/history structure is not automatically suitable for a developer tool.
